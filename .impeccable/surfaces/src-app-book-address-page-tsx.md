# Surface: the book (statement) — `/book/[address]`, and `/` showing the demo book

**Mode:** Read. A shared statement a skeptical reader checks. The owner's open flow lives on the same page.
**Audience and job:** a landlord, accountant or visa officer with no wallet confirms what a wallet was paid, across chains, and that nobody typed it. Judges follow any line to its origin, Lasna and callback transactions. The owner opens their book once.
**Proof on hand:** demo book `0xa7D5…7444` (3 lines, a refused look-alike, a refused forged write); docs/EVIDENCE.md.
**Memorable moment:** a payment prints onto the roll seconds after it's sent; the look-alike "USDC" sits struck through, unprinted.
**Open decisions:** wallet library for the open flow; how the three per-line transactions are shown on a phone.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The statement is a register roll: every payment prints itself as a numbered line on one roll, written by the machine, never by hand. It refuses the crypto dashboard (summary tiles, token icons, dark gradients) and the zebra-striped bank-portal table.
OWN-WORLD: A dark register-green counter is the frame. One thermal-white roll is the reading column, printed in near-black. A single roll-end pink marks only "live" and the roll's end. Torn top edge, dashed perforation rules, lines numbered No. 001 onward (raise: curved-crease plates). The newest line prints bright and the rest settle a tone (raise: brick arrival). Check state is a printed tick, strike or blank (raise: cutting-bench marks). Tabular figures on a decimal tab (raise: exposure record). The total is the page's only display figure (raise: precisionist numeral). Nothing outside paper, print, counter and the one accent (raise: hoarding palette). A calm grotesk for reading; mono only for labels and hashes.
STORY: The reader learns this wallet received N USDC across three named chains, sees that each line is ticked from its origin receipt, and opens any line's three transactions. The owner opens a book and watches Reactive confirm it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The counter's left rail holds the Annal mark, the thesis, the three watched chains and a live watching status. The centre roll (about a third of the width) holds the header (Income book, address, opened date), a "next payment prints here" edge, the newest lines first, the total as one large figure, and the struck look-alike. The right rail holds "Why you can trust it" and "Check any line". The owner's primary action, "Open my book", sits at the top of the roll when no book exists.
